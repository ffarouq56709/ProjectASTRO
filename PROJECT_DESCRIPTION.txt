Project ASTRO, Assistive Systems for Trusted Robotic Operations, is Faisal Farouq's solo, one-year build of a friendly caregiver-support research robot. The target concept is 1.829 m tall with rounded white panels, a connected-eye face and a warm voice. Year one aims for an operating mass of 55 kg or less, ten powered leg joints, two supervised three-step tethered trials at 0.10-0.15 m/s, and a separate stationary rig that moves a 2 kg inert object ten times. Software covers companionship, fictional care records, medication logging and approved mock messages. People stay in charge. No physical performance has been demonstrated. Qualified specialists must review and authorize the applicable later-stage work.
